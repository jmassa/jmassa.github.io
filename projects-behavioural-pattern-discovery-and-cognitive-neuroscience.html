Behavioural Pattern Discovery and Neuro-Cognitive Rehabilitation Tools

The goal of this project is the development of solutions based on Pattern Recognition Techniques, Computer Vision, and Artificial Intelligence for the application to Behavioural Recognition and the Development of Rehabilitation and Cognitive Stimulation Tools.
